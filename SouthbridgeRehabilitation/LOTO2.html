LOTO Procedure Checklist

  1. Notify affected employees that equipment will be shut down.
  2. Identify all hazardous energy sources connected to the equipment.
  3. Shut down the equipment using normal operating controls.
  4. Isolate all energy sources.
  5. Apply the authorized employee's lock and tag.
  6. Release, block, or restrain stored/residual energy.
  7. Verify isolation and zero-energy state before work begins.
  8. Perform the maintenance or service work.
  9. Inspect the area and make sure guards and components are restored.
  10. Remove locks/tags according to the facility's LOTO procedure.
  11. Notify affected employees and return equipment to service.
